Quality
WebP Quality
AVIF CRF
AVIF CPU-used
WebP Quality: 1 is the lowest quality/smallest output; 100 is maximum encoder quality. Browser WebP encoding does not guarantee mathematically lossless output at 100.
AVIF CRF: lower values produce higher quality and larger files; higher values produce lower quality and smaller files. Valid range: 1–63.
AVIF CPU-used: 0 is the slowest and most compression-efficient setting; 8 is the fastest. Higher values trade encoding time for compression efficiency.
